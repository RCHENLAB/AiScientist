===== SHARED — PI synthesis =====
**AGREED**

1. **Inference must be anchored to biological replicates (retinas/donors), not individual cells.**  
   *Reason:* Treating cells as independent observations inflates Type I error and misestimates power; genotype effects require organismal-level sampling units to avoid anti-conservative statistics.

2. **Multiple testing must use structured FDR (hierarchical/BH/IHW) rather than naive or rank-based methods.**  
   *Reason:* Per-class feature testing heavily inflates false discovery; structured correction calibrates error rates across classes and prevents overstatement of significance.

3. **Manuscript confidence must be explicitly tiered (high/moderate/low) and tied to replicate count, batch control, and validation status.**  
   *Reason:* Prevents overstatement when data is limited, conditional, or driven by technical artifacts; confidence must be calibrated to what the replicate structure and preprocessing actually support.

4. **Primary DDX41-driven transcriptional/splicing defects must be explicitly separated from secondary stress, glial reactivity, or apoptosis signatures.**  
   *Reason:* DDX41 loss triggers broad RNA processing stress; conflating primary mechanistic shifts with secondary degeneration leads to unwarranted causal claims.

5. **Mechanistic claims require orthogonal/junction-level validation; gene-level DE alone is insufficient.**  
   *Reason:* DDX41’s known roles in spliceosome maturation and R-loop resolution cannot be confirmed without PSI/junction quantification, smFISH, or damage foci; transcriptomic shifts must be framed as vulnerability unless validated.

===== SPLIT — PI synthesis =====
**AGREED**
1. **Decision:** Downgrade all inferential and mechanistic manuscript claims to exploratory/descriptive framing. **Reason:** The experimental design lacks biological replication (`n=1` per genotype), making biological variance unestimable and rendering p-values, FDRs, and pathway enrichments mathematically uninterpretable for population-level inference (converged on by all members).
2. **Decision:** Explicitly caveat the dataset’s restriction to 7 of 12 cell subclasses and avoid implying representativeness of the whole retina. **Reason:** Hard filtering systematically excluded 5 subclasses (12–38 cells per arm), creating non-random selection bias toward abundant or robustly captured populations; this limitation must be stated rather than implied (converged on by Biostatistician, Critic, and Bioinformatician’s scope admission).
3. **Decision:** Withhold any assertion that DDX41 drives retinal mitochondrial or splicing phenotypes until retina-specific validation is performed. **Reason:** Extrapolating from hematopoietic literature (`t6.csv`) is biologically unjustified, and single-case DE cannot distinguish mutant effects from individual genetic/epigenetic background (converged on by Biologist, Biostatistician, and Critic).
4. **Decision:** Require biological replication (≥2–3 per genotype) and orthogonal validation (pseudo-bulk DE, spatial transcriptomics, or functional assays) before elevating manuscript confidence. **Reason:** Without independent biological samples, no statistical framework can separate technical noise or individual-level variation from true genotype-driven signal (converged on by all members).
